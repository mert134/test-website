Make your report useful

A little context makes it much easier to understand and reproduce an issue.

  • Name the affected project.
  • List the steps that caused the issue.
  • Include the exact error text, if any.
◇

Keep private data private

Do not submit passwords, access keys, webhook URLs, or payment details. Remove sensitive information from screenshots before sharing them.

After submission, keep your reference code so the team can identify your report.

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Project and update information from the Zanity main site.

Visit the main site ↗
Why did the name change from Trollokoy to Zanity?

The new name was chosen because it looks sleeker and feels simpler.

Why do updates take a while?

Updates are planned before work begins, and a script for another game may take priority. There is no fixed release schedule.

Will Criminal vs SWAT and Trench War continue after v1.1?

The main site lists both at v1.0, with v1.1 waiting. Further updates are not guaranteed; v1.2 may be the final update.

Is Audio Hub available yet?

Not yet. Audio Hub is in development, and no release date has been announced.

Are more projects planned?

There is no fixed roadmap at the moment. New projects may be added as development progresses.

What should I include in a support report?

Include the project name, what you were doing, the exact error message, and steps to reproduce the issue. You can add contact information if you want a direct reply. Never include passwords, tokens, or webhook URLs.
